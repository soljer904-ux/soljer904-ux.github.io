3. Sensei Notes for Parents
  • Keep sessions short and upbeat: Ten minutes together while they still feel strong beats twenty minutes of fatigue. Stop on a win whenever you can.
  • Praise the method, not just speed: Asking "How did you work that one out?" builds real number sense.
  • Use the swap trick freely: If 7 × 4 feels hard, flip it to 4 × 7 and double twice.
  • Treat hints as smart training, not cheating: In karate you practise a kata slowly before doing it at full speed.
