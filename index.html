Crispy baked salmon goujons, sweet potato wedges & peas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Type
Fish
Tip
Check the strips for bones before crumbing. Flake a goujon for the twins if they find it easier.

Ingredients

  • 600g skinless salmon fillets, cut into finger-sized strips
  • 3 slices of bread (a day or two old), whizzed into crumbs
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 900g sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g plain Greek yoghurt (for the dip)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the sweet potato wedges in half the oil with a pinch of salt and roast for 30–35 min, turning once.
  2. Whizz the bread into crumbs, add the lemon zest and mix in the rest of the oil with your fingers.
  3. Put the flour, egg and crumbs in three bowls. Dip each salmon strip in flour, then egg, then crumbs, and lay on a lined tray.
  4. Bake the goujons for the last 12–15 min of the wedges' time, until golden and the salmon flakes easily.
  5. Boil the peas for 3 min. Mix the yoghurt with a squeeze of lemon for dipping.

Salmon strips in golden homemade breadcrumbs, baked not fried, with sweet potato wedges, peas and a lemony yoghurt dip. Like posh fish fingers.

Shop-bought chicken Kievs (baked from frozen) with new potatoes and peas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Chicken

Ingredients

  • About 8 frozen garlic chicken Kievs (2 packs of 4)
  • 750g baby new potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas (from the freezer)
  • Salad leaves to serve (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to the temperature on the Kiev pack (usually about 200°C / 180°C fan).
  2. Put the frozen Kievs on a baking tray and bake for the time on the pack (usually 25–30 min), until piping hot all the way through.
  3. Meanwhile boil the new potatoes for about 15 min until tender.
  4. Cook the peas from frozen in boiling water for 3 min, then drain.
  5. Serve the Kievs with the potatoes and peas. Let the Kievs sit for a minute — the garlic butter inside is very hot.

Frozen garlic chicken Kievs baked from frozen, with boiled new potatoes and peas. No homemade crumb or garlic butter.

Marry Me Chicken rice

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Type
Chicken
Tip
Lighter plate: use single cream, extra spinach and a big pile of green veg beside a smaller bit of rice. The sauce freezes well if you make a double batch.

Ingredients

  • 4–5 chicken breasts (about 700–800g), halved sideways into thinner steaks
  • 300g basmati or long-grain rice
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 100g sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and chopped (keep 1 tbsp of the oil)
  • 200ml chicken stock
  • 200ml double cream (or single cream for a lighter sauce)
  • 60g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (or dried oregano)
  • Pinch of chilli flakes (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (if you need more than the tomato oil)
  • Handful of baby spinach (optional)
  • Fresh basil to serve (optional)
  • Green veg or salad on the side (broccoli or beans work well)

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the pack and keep warm. Steam or boil the green veg if using.
  2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Heat the sun-dried tomato oil (plus a little olive oil if needed) in a large wide pan over a medium-high heat. Brown the chicken 3–4 min each side until golden; it doesn't need to be cooked through yet. Lift out onto a plate.
  3. Turn the heat down a little. Soften the onion in the same pan for 3–4 min, then add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 1 min.
  4. Pour in the stock and cream, stir in the Italian seasoning and a pinch of chilli flakes if using, and simmer 3–4 min until slightly thickened.
  5. Stir in the Parmesan until melted. Return the chicken (and any juices) to the pan, spoon the sauce over, and simmer gently 8–10 min until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Wilt in the spinach if using. Slice the chicken into strips for the little ones and serve over the rice with plenty of sauce and the green veg. Scatter basil if you have it.

Tender chicken in a creamy sun-dried tomato and Parmesan sauce, served over rice. Family-friendly — keep the chilli flakes optional.

Baked chicken katsu with curry sauce

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: more chicken and broccoli, a small spoon of rice, and the sauce on the side.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Sauce: 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped
  • Sauce: 2 garlic cloves
  • Sauce: 1 tbsp mild curry powder and 1 tbsp plain flour
  • Sauce: 1 chicken stock cube in 400ml boiling water
  • Sauce: 1 tsp honey and 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 300g rice
  • 1 head broccoli

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Slice each chicken breast in half sideways so it's thin.
  2. Put flour, egg and panko in three bowls. Dip each piece in flour, then egg, then panko.
  3. Lay on a lined tray, drizzle with the oil and bake 20–22 min, turning once, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Sauce: soften the onion and carrot in a little oil for 8 min, then add the garlic, curry powder and flour for 1 min.
  5. Slowly stir in the stock and simmer 10 min. Blend smooth with a stick blender, then stir in the honey and soy.
  6. Cook the rice and steam the broccoli. Slice the chicken and serve with the sauce.

Hidden-veg tomato pasta bake

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
45 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a smaller portion with a big side salad. Stir leftover cooked chicken through the adult portion for extra protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, 2 peppers, 1 courgette, 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 400g tin cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 veg stock cube
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 300g penne
  • 125g mozzarella ball, torn, and 50g cheddar, grated

Method

  1. Soften the veg in the oil in a big pan for 10 min. Add the garlic for 1 min.
  2. Add the tomatoes, beans, stock cube and basil. Simmer 10 min.
  3. Blend smooth with a stick blender, so the beans and veg disappear.
  4. Cook the pasta 2 min less than the packet says, then drain.
  5. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Mix the pasta and sauce in a baking dish and top with the cheeses.
  6. Bake 20 min until bubbling.

Chinese-style beef & pepper stir-fry

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
25 min
Type
Mince
Tip
Can't handle the heat? Leave the chilli off the kids' plates or serve it in a little bowl on the side. Dry sherry works if you haven't got Chinese rice wine.

Ingredients

  • 500g lean beef mince (from the freezer)
  • 300g white long grain rice
  • 2 green peppers, diced
  • 2 brown onions, diced
  • 6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 40g fresh root ginger, finely chopped
  • 2 red chillies, sliced into rounds (optional garnish)
  • 75ml Chinese rice wine (or dry sherry)
  • 100g hoisin sauce
  • 75ml soy sauce
  • 25ml toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp black pepper (freshly ground)
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Method

  1. Add the rice to a pot with a lid with about 500ml cold water and bring to the boil. Reduce to very low and cook, covered, for 12–15 min until the water has absorbed. Take off the heat and leave covered to steam.
  2. Meanwhile peel and dice the onions. Deseed and dice the peppers.
  3. Heat a large wide pan with a drizzle of vegetable oil over a medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook 3–5 min until beginning to soften.
  4. Finely chop (or grate) the garlic and ginger.
  5. Turn the heat to high, add the mince and cook about 5 min until browned all over, breaking it up as you go. Stir in the black pepper while it browns.
  6. Add the pepper, garlic and ginger and cook 2 min more. Reduce to medium, then add the Chinese rice wine, hoisin, soy sauce, sesame oil and sugar. Cook 3–4 min until thickened and sticky and the mince is cooked through.
  7. Serve the stir-fry over the rice. Garnish with chilli rounds (go easy for the little ones) and a grind of black pepper.

Gousto's Chinese-style beef & green pepper stir-fry with rice: mince in a sticky hoisin sauce. Scaled for 5 from the 2-person recipe; uses the freezer mince.

Steak (grown-ups) & breaded chicken (kids) with salad & Tenderstem

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
30 min
Type
Beef
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller steak, extra salad and Tenderstem.

Ingredients

  • 2 Lidl steaks (sirloin or rump, about 400g total), for the grown-ups
  • 1 pack shop-bought breaded chicken mini fillets or goujons (about 400g), for the kids
  • Tenderstem broccoli (about 200g)
  • 1 head broccoli (optional extra greens)
  • Salad leaves, cherry tomatoes, cucumber
  • Butter and a garlic clove for basting (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven for the breaded chicken and cook it for the time on the pack until piping hot all the way through.
  2. Steam or boil the Tenderstem (and broccoli if using) for about 5 min until just tender.
  3. Take the steaks out of the fridge 20 min before cooking. Pat dry and season.
  4. Heat a heavy pan until very hot. Cook the steaks 2–3 min each side for medium-rare, adding butter and garlic to baste at the end.
  5. Rest 5 min while you toss the salad. Serve steak with salad and greens; kids get the breaded chicken with the same sides.

Lidl steak, pan-fried, for the grown-ups; shop-bought breaded chicken for the kids; a big crunchy salad and Tenderstem broccoli. Leaner sides than the usual wedges.

Chicken & leek pie (pastry lid only)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
50 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a big scoop of filling with a small piece of pastry, and a pile of greens. Skip the potatoes.
Tip
The filling can be made in the morning and kept in the fridge. Just add the pastry and bake later.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts (about 600g), diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 150g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 200ml boiling water
  • 300ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 sheet (320g) ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Broccoli or green beans to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Fry the chicken in the oil in a large pan for 5 min until white all over.
  3. Add the leeks and mushrooms and cook 5 min until soft.
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 min.
  5. Slowly stir in the stock, then the milk. Simmer 5 min, stirring, until thick. Add the thyme and peas.
  6. Tip into an ovenproof dish. Lay the pastry over the top, trim the edges, brush with egg and cut a small slit.
  7. Bake 25 min until golden. Serve with greens (and mash or new potatoes for the kids).

Oven-baked chicken goujons

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a generous portion of goujons with lots of salad and veg, a few wedges, and the yoghurt dip.
Tip
Baked, not fried. Make extra crumbed strips and freeze them raw on the tray, then bag up for another night (bake from frozen, 25 min).

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts, cut into finger-width strips
  • 3 slices of bread (a day or two old), whizzed into crumbs, or 100g panko
  • 1 tsp mild paprika (optional, for colour)
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 600g potatoes, cut into wedges (skin on)
  • Sides: frozen peas and sweetcorn
  • Sides: cucumber, cherry tomatoes and carrot sticks
  • Dip: 150g Greek yoghurt with a squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the wedges in a little of the oil and roast on a tray for 35 min, turning once.
  2. Whizz the bread into crumbs (or use panko) and mix with the paprika and a pinch of salt. Mix the remaining oil through the crumbs with your fingers.
  3. Put the flour, egg and crumbs in three bowls. Dip each chicken strip in flour, then egg, then crumbs, and lay on a lined tray with gaps between them.
  4. Bake for 18–20 min, turning once, until golden. Cut the thickest one open to check there's no pink.
  5. Meanwhile cook the peas and sweetcorn, and chop the cucumber, tomatoes and carrots.
  6. Serve the goujons with the wedges, veg and yoghurt dip.
