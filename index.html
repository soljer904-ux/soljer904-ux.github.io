Slow cooker chicken curry

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
15 min prep + 6–8 hr low
Lighter adult plate
Adults: lots of curry with a small spoon of rice (or half rice, half cauliflower rice) and an extra dollop of yoghurt.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes or a chopped fresh chilli on top.
Tip
Prep it before the school run and it looks after itself.

Ingredients

  • 800g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut in chunks
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 150ml boiling water
  • 150g frozen spinach or peas
  • 150g plain Greek yoghurt
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt
  • 300g basmati rice

Method

  1. In the morning put the chicken, onion, garlic, ginger, curry powder and garam masala into the slow cooker and stir to coat.
  2. Add the tomatoes, purée, stock and a pinch of salt. Stir.
  3. Lid on, cook on low for 6–8 hr.
  4. 20 min before eating, stir in the frozen spinach or peas.
  5. Cook the rice as the packet says.
  6. Turn off the heat and stir in the yoghurt and lemon juice just before serving (the yoghurt can split if it boils).

Steak (grown-ups) & breaded chicken (kids) with Tenderstem & potato dauphinoise

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
30 min
Type
Beef
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller steak, a small spoon of dauphinoise and extra Tenderstem.

Ingredients

  • 2 Lidl sirloin steaks (about 400g total), for the grown-ups
  • 1 pack breaded chicken mini fillets or goujons (about 400g), for the kids
  • Tenderstem broccoli (about 200g)
  • Leftover potato dauphinoise from the freezer (defrost in the fridge on Sunday morning; don't buy any)
  • Butter and a garlic clove for basting (optional)

Method

  1. Defrost the dauphinoise in the fridge in the morning.
  2. Reheat the dauphinoise, covered with foil, in the oven at 180°C (160°C fan) for about 25–30 min until piping hot, taking the foil off for the last 5 min.
  3. Cook the breaded chicken in the same oven for the time on the pack until piping hot all the way through.
  4. Take the steaks out of the fridge 20 min before cooking. Pat dry and season.
  5. Heat a heavy pan until very hot. Cook the steaks 2–3 min each side for medium-rare, adding butter and garlic to baste at the end.
  6. Rest 5 min while you steam the Tenderstem for about 4 min.

Lidl steak, pan-fried, for the grown-ups; oven-baked breaded chicken for the kids; Tenderstem and the leftover potato dauphinoise from the freezer (don't buy any).

Sausage, leek & mushroom orzo (one pot)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a smaller scoop with extra sausage on top and a big handful of rocket or salad.
Tip
If it gets too thick, add a splash of boiling water.

Ingredients

  • 8 Tesco Finest pork sausages (the family's usual)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 200g mushrooms (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 250g orzo
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 900ml boiling water
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 2 handfuls spinach
  • 40g parmesan or cheddar, grated
  • Zest and juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the skins in small chunks.
  2. Brown the chunks in the oil in a wide pan for 5–6 min until golden.
  3. Add the leeks and mushrooms and cook 5 min. Add the garlic and thyme for 1 min.
  4. Stir in the orzo and Dijon, then pour in the stock.
  5. Simmer 12–15 min, stirring often so it doesn't stick, until the orzo is soft and most liquid is gone.
  6. Stir in the peas and spinach for the last 3 min, then the cheese, lemon zest and juice, and salt and pepper to taste.

One-tray chicken & veg traybake

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
50 min (10 min hands-on)
Lighter adult plate
Adults: 2 thighs, lots of veg and broccoli, and a few potatoes.
Tip
It's all one tray, which makes it the easiest Thursday meal.

Ingredients

  • 8 boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • Autumn/winter: 600g potatoes or sweet potatoes, 2 carrots, 1 red onion
  • Spring/summer: 600g new potatoes, 1 courgette, 1 pepper, 1 red onion
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Chop the veg (not the broccoli) into chunks and spread on a large tray.
  3. Add the chicken, oil, herbs, garlic, smoked paprika, salt and pepper and toss everything together with your hands. Squeeze over half the lemon.
  4. Roast 25 min.
  5. Add the broccoli, stir and roast 15–20 min more, until the chicken is golden and cooked through and the veg is golden. Serve with the rest of the lemon in wedges.

Mild chilli con carne

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
45 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a big bowl of chilli over lettuce with a small spoon of rice and yoghurt on top.
Adult add-on
Hot sauce or chilli flakes in adult bowls.
Tip
In summer, serve it as "chilli bowls" with lettuce, cucumber and sweetcorn.

Ingredients

  • 500g lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red pepper, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp mild chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 1 beef stock cube in 150ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp red wine vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 300g rice
  • Greek yoghurt and grated cheddar to serve

Method

  1. Brown the mince in a large pan for 6–8 min until well browned.
  2. Add the onion and pepper and cook 5 min. Add the garlic and spices for 1 min.
  3. Add the tomatoes, purée, beans, stock and Worcestershire sauce.
  4. Simmer 25 min with the lid off, stirring now and then, until thick. Stir in the vinegar and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook the rice.
  6. Serve with a spoon of yoghurt and a little cheese.

Creamy salmon, leek & potato traybake

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Fish
Tip
Lighter plate: a whole fillet with plenty of peas and Tenderstem, and fewer potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 3 salmon fillets (about 360g)
  • 350g baby potatoes, thickly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 leeks, halved, washed and sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 150ml half-fat crème fraîche
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard and the zest and juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp chives (fresh or dried)
  • To serve: frozen peas and Tenderstem broccoli

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Boil the potatoes for 8 min, drain and leave to steam-dry for a few minutes.
  2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil, salt and pepper in a roasting tin and roast for 20 min, tossing halfway.
  3. Meanwhile, fry the leeks in the rest of the oil over a medium heat for 5–6 min until soft and just golden, then stir in the garlic for 1 min.
  4. Turn the heat down low and stir in the crème fraîche, Dijon, lemon zest and about 100ml hot water until smooth and just bubbling (keep it gentle so it doesn't split). Stir in the chives and season.
  5. Heat the grill to high. Pour the creamy leeks over the potatoes, sit the salmon on top, season and grill for 7–8 min until the salmon flakes easily. Squeeze over the lemon juice.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the peas and Tenderstem, and serve on the side.

Golden roast baby potatoes in a mild, creamy leek sauce with salmon on top, served with peas and Tenderstem. Scaled for 4 and made with half-fat crème fraîche, with Dijon and lemon for flavour.

Chicken fajitas (homemade spice mix)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
25 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a fajita bowl, with chicken and peppers on lettuce, plus yoghurt and salsa and one wrap or none.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes or hot sauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts, sliced into strips
  • 2 peppers and 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Spice mix: 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp salt
  • 8 large tortilla wraps (Tesco Plain White Tortilla Wraps 8 Pack)
  • 1 little gem lettuce, 2 tomatoes, ¼ cucumber
  • Grated cheddar and Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lime

Method

  1. Mix the spices in a bowl and toss the chicken in them.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken 6–8 min until golden and cooked through.
  3. Add the peppers and onion and cook 5 min until soft. Squeeze over half the lime.
  4. Chop the lettuce, tomatoes and cucumber.
  5. Warm the wraps in the microwave for 20 seconds.
  6. Put everything on the table and let everyone build their own.

Baked chicken katsu with curry sauce

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: more chicken and broccoli, a small spoon of rice, and the sauce on the side.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • A pinch of salt
  • Sauce: 1 onion and 2 carrots, chopped
  • Sauce: 2 garlic cloves
  • Sauce: 1 tbsp mild curry powder, ½ tsp ground ginger and 1 tbsp plain flour
  • Sauce: 1 tsp tomato purée
  • Sauce: 1 chicken stock cube in 400ml boiling water
  • Sauce: 1 tsp honey and 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 300g rice
  • 1 head broccoli

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Slice each chicken breast in half sideways so it's thin.
  2. Put flour, egg and panko in three bowls. Dip each piece in flour, then egg, then panko.
  3. Lay on a lined tray, season with a pinch of salt, drizzle with the oil and bake 20–22 min, turning once, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Sauce: soften the onion and carrot in a little oil for 8 min, then add the garlic, curry powder, ginger, tomato purée and flour for 1 min.
  5. Slowly stir in the stock and simmer 10 min. Blend smooth with a stick blender, then stir in the honey and soy. Taste and add a pinch of salt if it needs it.
  6. Cook the rice and steam the broccoli. Slice the chicken and serve with the sauce.

Freezer chicken Kievs with baby potatoes & peas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Type
Chicken
Tip
Lighter plate: one Kiev, plenty of peas and a small spoon of potatoes.

Ingredients

  • Garlic chicken Kievs from the freezer (1 per grown-up, and 1 or 2 shared between the kids)
  • 400g baby potatoes
  • 200g frozen peas
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Cook the Kievs from frozen as the pack says (usually about 30–35 min at 200°C / 180°C fan).
  2. Boil the baby potatoes for 15–20 min until tender, drain and lightly crush with a fork, a little Flora and pepper.
  3. Cook the peas for the last 3 min.
  4. Serve with a squeeze of lemon.
