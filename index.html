Chicken & leek pie (pastry lid only)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
50 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a big scoop of filling with a small piece of pastry, and a pile of greens. Skip the potatoes.
Tip
The filling can be made in the morning and kept in the fridge. Just add the pastry and bake later.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts (about 600g), diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 150g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 200ml boiling water
  • 300ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 sheet (320g) ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Broccoli or green beans to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Fry the chicken in the oil in a large pan for 5 min until white all over.
  3. Add the leeks and mushrooms and cook 5 min until soft.
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 min.
  5. Slowly stir in the stock, then the milk. Simmer 5 min, stirring, until thick. Add the thyme and peas.
  6. Tip into an ovenproof dish. Lay the pastry over the top, trim the edges, brush with egg and cut a small slit.
  7. Bake 25 min until golden. Serve with greens (and mash or new potatoes for the kids).

Creamy salmon, leek & potato traybake

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Fish
Tip
Lighter plate: a whole fillet with plenty of peas and broccoli, and fewer potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 500g baby potatoes, thickly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 leeks, halved, washed and sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200ml reduced-fat crème fraîche (instead of the recipe's double cream)
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped (fresh or dried)
  • 5 skinless salmon fillets
  • To serve: frozen peas and broccoli

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Boil the potatoes for 8 min, drain and leave to steam-dry for a few minutes.
  2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and some seasoning in a large roasting tin and roast for 20 min, tossing halfway.
  3. Meanwhile, fry the leeks in the rest of the oil over a medium heat for 5 min until soft, then stir in the garlic for 1 min.
  4. Turn the heat down low and stir in the crème fraîche with about 150ml hot water until smooth and just bubbling (keep it gentle so it doesn't split). Stir through the chives.
  5. Heat the grill to high. Pour the creamy leeks over the potatoes, sit the salmon on top and grill for 7–8 min until the salmon flakes easily.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the peas and broccoli, and serve on the side.

Golden roast baby potatoes in a mild, creamy leek sauce with salmon on top, served with peas and broccoli. Made with reduced-fat crème fraîche instead of double cream, and scaled up for 5.

Hidden-veg spaghetti bolognese

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a big ladle of sauce over a small nest of pasta (or courgette ribbons) with a side salad. The sauce is the protein, so be generous with it.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes stirred into adult bowls.
Tip
Make double and freeze half. It's the family's most-cooked meal.

Ingredients

  • 500g lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 courgette
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g spaghetti
  • Grated cheddar to serve

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion, grate the carrot and courgette, crush the garlic.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pan and soften the onion for 5 min.
  3. Add the mince and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook 5–6 min until browned all over.
  4. Stir in the garlic, carrot and courgette and cook 2 min.
  5. Add the tomatoes, purée, crumbled stock cube and oregano. Simmer 20 min with the lid off, stirring now and then.
  6. Meanwhile cook the spaghetti as the packet says. Snap it shorter or cut it up for the twins.
  7. Serve topped with a little cheese.

Sausage & bean casserole

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
50 min (or slow cooker 6 hr low)
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a bowl of casserole with extra greens and no mash. The beans and sausages carry the protein.
Adult add-on
A pinch of chilli flakes.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken sausages
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 1 sweet potato, peeled and cubed
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (mild)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 400g tin butter beans or cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 300ml boiling water

Method

  1. Brown the sausages in the oil in a big lidded pan for 5 min. Lift out and cut each in half.
  2. Soften the onion, carrot and pepper in the same pan for 5 min.
  3. Stir in the garlic and paprika for 1 min.
  4. Add the sweet potato, tomatoes, beans, stock, thyme and sausages.
  5. Lid on and simmer gently for 30 min until the sweet potato is soft. Or put everything in the slow cooker on low for 6 hr.
  6. Serve with mash or crusty bread for the kids.

Homemade flatbread pizzas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
20 min
Type
Other
Tip
Lighter plate: a thinner wrap base loaded with veg and chicken, with a big salad on the side.

Ingredients

  • 5 flatbreads or large wholemeal wraps
  • 200ml passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 balls mozzarella, torn (or 150g grated)
  • Toppings: sliced ham or cooked chicken, peppers, sweetcorn, cherry tomatoes, mushrooms
  • Rocket or a big side salad

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and put two trays in to heat.
  2. Simmer the passata with the garlic and oregano for 5 min until a bit thicker.
  3. Spread a thin layer of sauce on each flatbread, then add mozzarella and toppings.
  4. Slide onto the hot trays and bake 8–10 min until bubbling and crisp at the edges.
  5. Cut into strips for the twins and serve with salad.

Fakeaway pizza night, made from scratch on flatbreads with a quick passata sauce.

Big family lasagne with salad

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
2 hr 15 min (make ahead, then 50 min to bake)
Type
Mince
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller square of lasagne with a big pile of salad.

Ingredients

  • 1.2kg lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, finely grated
  • 2 celery sticks, finely chopped
  • 1 courgette, finely grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 500g passata
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 beef stock cube made up with 200ml hot water
  • 2 tsp dried oregano and 2 bay leaves
  • About 400g dried lasagne sheets
  • White sauce: 1.2 litres semi-skimmed milk, 70g butter, 70g plain flour, a grating of nutmeg
  • 100g mature cheddar, grated, plus 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 x 125g ball mozzarella, torn
  • Big salad: 2 bags mixed leaves, 250g cherry tomatoes, 1 cucumber, 2 peppers, 2 carrots cut into ribbons

Method

  1. Ragù: brown the mince in a large pan in two batches, then tip out any fat. Add the onions, carrots, celery and courgette and cook 8–10 min until soft, then add the garlic for 1 min.
  2. Stir in the tomato purée, chopped tomatoes, passata, stock, oregano and bay. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 45 min until thick. Take out the bay leaves.
  3. White sauce: melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 min, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer, whisking, 5 min until it coats a spoon. Season with nutmeg and stir in half the cheddar.
  4. Assemble in a large deep roasting tin (about 30 x 40cm) or two medium dishes: a thin layer of ragù, then sheets, ragù, white sauce. Repeat to make 3 layers, finishing with white sauce, then the mozzarella, the rest of the cheddar and the parmesan.
  5. Make ahead: cool, cover and chill (morning or the day before). To serve, bake at 190°C (170°C fan) for 45–50 min (about 10 min less if not chilled) until bubbling and golden. Rest 10 min before cutting.
  6. Toss the salad just before serving and cut smaller squares for the little ones.

From-scratch lasagne for a crowd: lean beef ragù packed with hidden veg, a homemade white sauce and a golden cheesy top, with a big crunchy salad. Makes 10–12 portions, so there's some left over.

Steak (grown-ups) & breaded chicken (kids) with sweet potato wedges

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Beef
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller steak, extra salad and broccoli, and a few wedges.

Ingredients

  • 2 Lidl steaks (sirloin or rump, about 400g total), for the grown-ups
  • 1 pack shop-bought breaded chicken mini fillets or goujons (about 400g), for the kids
  • 3 large sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Tenderstem broccoli
  • Salad leaves, cherry tomatoes, cucumber
  • Butter and a garlic clove for basting (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the wedges in the oil, season and roast 30–35 min, turning once.
  2. Put the breaded chicken in the oven for the time on the pack, timed to finish with the wedges.
  3. Take the steaks out of the fridge 20 min before cooking. Pat dry and season.
  4. Heat a heavy pan until very hot. Cook the steaks 2–3 min each side for medium-rare, adding butter and garlic to baste at the end.
  5. Rest 5 min while you steam the broccoli and toss the salad. Check the chicken is piping hot all the way through before serving.

Lidl steak, pan-fried, for the grown-ups; shop-bought breaded chicken for the kids; oven-baked sweet potato wedges, Tenderstem broccoli and a side salad.

Oven-baked chicken goujons

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a generous portion of goujons with lots of salad and veg, a few wedges, and the yoghurt dip.
Tip
Baked, not fried. Make extra crumbed strips and freeze them raw on the tray, then bag up for another night (bake from frozen, 25 min).

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts, cut into finger-width strips
  • 3 slices of bread (a day or two old), whizzed into crumbs, or 100g panko
  • 1 tsp mild paprika (optional, for colour)
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 600g potatoes, cut into wedges (skin on)
  • Sides: frozen peas and sweetcorn
  • Sides: cucumber, cherry tomatoes and carrot sticks
  • Dip: 150g Greek yoghurt with a squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the wedges in a little of the oil and roast on a tray for 35 min, turning once.
  2. Whizz the bread into crumbs (or use panko) and mix with the paprika and a pinch of salt. Mix the remaining oil through the crumbs with your fingers.
  3. Put the flour, egg and crumbs in three bowls. Dip each chicken strip in flour, then egg, then crumbs, and lay on a lined tray with gaps between them.
  4. Bake for 18–20 min, turning once, until golden. Cut the thickest one open to check there's no pink.
  5. Meanwhile cook the peas and sweetcorn, and chop the cucumber, tomatoes and carrots.
  6. Serve the goujons with the wedges, veg and yoghurt dip.

Oven-baked chicken schnitzel

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a whole schnitzel with plenty of salad and greens and just a few new potatoes.
Tip
Bashing the chicken thin is the trick: it cooks quickly and evenly. You can crumb them in the morning and keep them covered in the fridge until tea time.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 100g panko or homemade breadcrumbs (3 slices of bread, whizzed)
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • Zest of 1 lemon (lemon wedges to serve)
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 500g baby new potatoes
  • 2 heads broccoli or 300g green beans
  • Salad: 1 little gem lettuce, cucumber and cherry tomatoes

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and put a lightly oiled baking tray in to heat.
  2. Boil the new potatoes for 15–20 min until tender.
  3. Lay each chicken breast between two sheets of baking paper and bash with a rolling pin until about 1cm thick all over.
  4. Mix the crumbs with the herbs, lemon zest and a pinch of salt. Put the flour, egg and crumbs in three bowls. Dip each breast in flour, then egg, then crumbs.
  5. Put the chicken on the hot tray, drizzle with the oil and bake 10 min, turn, then 8–10 min more until golden and cooked through.
  6. Steam the broccoli or beans and chop the salad. Slice the schnitzels into strips for the little ones and serve with lemon wedges.

Apple & oat muffins (snack)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Snack
Tip
Keeps 3 days in a tin, or freeze and defrost overnight.

Ingredients

  • 2 apples, grated (about 200g)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 160g rolled oats
  • About 30g honey
  • A splash of milk if the mix is too dry

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and grease or line a 12-hole muffin tin.
  2. Grate the apples and mix them with the eggs, oats and honey until it holds together. Add a splash of milk if it looks dry.
  3. Spoon into the tin and bake for about 30 minutes until set and golden.
  4. Cool slightly before serving.

Makes 12. About 90 kcal each. Great for lunchboxes or after school.
