Homemade flatbread pizzas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
20 min
Type
Other
Tip
Lighter plate: a thinner wrap base loaded with veg and chicken, with a big salad on the side.

Ingredients

  • 5 flatbreads or large wholemeal wraps
  • 200ml passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 balls mozzarella, torn (or 150g grated)
  • Toppings: sliced ham or cooked chicken, peppers, sweetcorn, cherry tomatoes, mushrooms
  • Rocket or a big side salad

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and put two trays in to heat.
  2. Simmer the passata with the garlic and oregano for 5 min until a bit thicker.
  3. Spread a thin layer of sauce on each flatbread, then add mozzarella and toppings.
  4. Slide onto the hot trays and bake 8–10 min until bubbling and crisp at the edges.
  5. Cut into strips for the twins and serve with salad.

Fakeaway pizza night, made from scratch on flatbreads with a quick passata sauce.

Big family lasagne with salad

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
2 hr 15 min (make ahead, then 50 min to bake)
Type
Mince
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller square of lasagne with a big pile of salad.

Ingredients

  • 1.2kg lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, finely grated
  • 2 celery sticks, finely chopped
  • 1 courgette, finely grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 500g passata
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 beef stock cube made up with 200ml hot water
  • 2 tsp dried oregano and 2 bay leaves
  • About 400g dried lasagne sheets
  • White sauce: 1.2 litres semi-skimmed milk, 70g butter, 70g plain flour, a grating of nutmeg
  • 100g mature cheddar, grated, plus 30g parmesan, grated
  • 1 x 125g ball mozzarella, torn
  • Big salad: 2 bags mixed leaves, 250g cherry tomatoes, 1 cucumber, 2 peppers, 2 carrots cut into ribbons

Method

  1. Ragù: brown the mince in a large pan in two batches, then tip out any fat. Add the onions, carrots, celery and courgette and cook 8–10 min until soft, then add the garlic for 1 min.
  2. Stir in the tomato purée, chopped tomatoes, passata, stock, oregano and bay. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 45 min until thick. Take out the bay leaves.
  3. White sauce: melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 min, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer, whisking, 5 min until it coats a spoon. Season with nutmeg and stir in half the cheddar.
  4. Assemble in a large deep roasting tin (about 30 x 40cm) or two medium dishes: a thin layer of ragù, then sheets, ragù, white sauce. Repeat to make 3 layers, finishing with white sauce, then the mozzarella, the rest of the cheddar and the parmesan.
  5. Make ahead: cool, cover and chill (morning or the day before). To serve, bake at 190°C (170°C fan) for 45–50 min (about 10 min less if not chilled) until bubbling and golden. Rest 10 min before cutting.
  6. Toss the salad just before serving and cut smaller squares for the little ones.

From-scratch lasagne for a crowd: lean beef ragù packed with hidden veg, a homemade white sauce and a golden cheesy top, with a big crunchy salad. Makes 10–12 portions, so there's some left over.

Steak (grown-ups) & breaded chicken (kids) with sweet potato wedges

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Beef
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller steak, extra salad and broccoli, and a few wedges.

Ingredients

  • 2 Lidl steaks (sirloin or rump, about 400g total), for the grown-ups
  • 1 pack shop-bought breaded chicken mini fillets or goujons (about 400g), for the kids
  • 3 large sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Tenderstem broccoli
  • Salad leaves, cherry tomatoes, cucumber
  • Butter and a garlic clove for basting (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the wedges in the oil, season and roast 30–35 min, turning once.
  2. Put the breaded chicken in the oven for the time on the pack, timed to finish with the wedges.
  3. Take the steaks out of the fridge 20 min before cooking. Pat dry and season.
  4. Heat a heavy pan until very hot. Cook the steaks 2–3 min each side for medium-rare, adding butter and garlic to baste at the end.
  5. Rest 5 min while you steam the broccoli and toss the salad. Check the chicken is piping hot all the way through before serving.

Lidl steak, pan-fried, for the grown-ups; shop-bought breaded chicken for the kids; oven-baked sweet potato wedges, Tenderstem broccoli and a side salad.

Chicken fajitas (homemade spice mix)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
25 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a fajita bowl, with chicken and peppers on lettuce, plus yoghurt and salsa and one wrap or none.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes or hot sauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts, sliced into strips
  • 2 peppers and 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Spice mix: 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 8–10 small tortilla wraps
  • 1 little gem lettuce, 2 tomatoes, ¼ cucumber
  • Grated cheddar and Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lime

Method

  1. Mix the spices in a bowl and toss the chicken in them.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken 6–8 min until cooked through.
  3. Add the peppers and onion and cook 5 min until soft. Squeeze over half the lime.
  4. Chop the lettuce, tomatoes and cucumber.
  5. Warm the wraps in the microwave for 20 seconds.
  6. Put everything on the table and let everyone build their own.

Sausage, leek & mushroom orzo (one pot)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a smaller scoop with extra sausage on top and a big handful of rocket or salad.
Tip
If it gets too thick, add a splash of boiling water.

Ingredients

  • 8 Tesco Finest pork sausages (the family's usual)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 200g mushrooms (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 250g orzo
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 900ml boiling water
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 2 handfuls spinach
  • 40g parmesan or cheddar, grated

Method

  1. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the skins in small chunks.
  2. Brown the chunks in the oil in a wide pan for 5 min.
  3. Add the leeks and mushrooms and cook 5 min. Add the garlic for 1 min.
  4. Stir in the orzo, then pour in the stock.
  5. Simmer 12–15 min, stirring often so it doesn't stick, until the orzo is soft and most liquid is gone.
  6. Stir in the peas and spinach for the last 3 min, then the cheese.

Cottage pie

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
1 hr
Lighter adult plate
Adults: more of the meat layer, a thin layer of potato mash, and green veg on the side.
Tip
Can be assembled in the morning and baked later. Add 10 min if it's from the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 500g lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 150g mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 beef stock cube in 300ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1.2kg Maris Piper potatoes
  • Knob of butter and a splash of milk
  • 50g cheddar, grated

Method

  1. Peel and chop the potatoes and boil 15–20 min until soft. Drain and mash with butter and milk.
  2. Meanwhile brown the mince in a large pan for 5 min. Add the onion, carrot and mushrooms and cook 5 min.
  3. Stir in the flour and tomato purée for 1 min.
  4. Add the stock, Worcestershire sauce and thyme. Simmer 15 min until thick, then stir in the peas.
  5. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Spoon the mince into a dish, spread the mash on top and sprinkle with cheese.
  6. Bake 25 min until golden and bubbling.

Slow cooker chicken curry

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
15 min prep + 6–8 hr low
Lighter adult plate
Adults: lots of curry with a small spoon of rice (or half rice, half cauliflower rice) and an extra dollop of yoghurt.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes or a chopped fresh chilli on top.
Tip
Prep it before the school run and it looks after itself.

Ingredients

  • 800g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut in chunks
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 2 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 150ml boiling water
  • 150g frozen spinach or peas
  • 150g plain Greek yoghurt
  • 300g basmati rice

Method

  1. In the morning put the chicken, onion, garlic, ginger and curry powder into the slow cooker and stir to coat.
  2. Add the tomatoes, purée and stock. Stir.
  3. Lid on, cook on low for 6–8 hr.
  4. 20 min before eating, stir in the frozen spinach or peas.
  5. Cook the rice as the packet says.
  6. Turn off the heat and stir in the yoghurt just before serving (it can split if it boils).

Crispy baked salmon goujons, sweet potato wedges & peas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
35 min
Type
Fish
Tip
Check the strips for bones before crumbing. Flake a goujon for the twins if they find it easier.

Ingredients

  • 600g skinless salmon fillets, cut into finger-sized strips
  • 3 slices of bread (a day or two old), whizzed into crumbs
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 900g sweet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 1 lemon
  • 100g plain Greek yoghurt (for the dip)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the sweet potato wedges in half the oil with a pinch of salt and roast for 30–35 min, turning once.
  2. Whizz the bread into crumbs, add the lemon zest and mix in the rest of the oil with your fingers.
  3. Put the flour, egg and crumbs in three bowls. Dip each salmon strip in flour, then egg, then crumbs, and lay on a lined tray.
  4. Bake the goujons for the last 12–15 min of the wedges' time, until golden and the salmon flakes easily.
  5. Boil the peas for 3 min. Mix the yoghurt with a squeeze of lemon for dipping.

Salmon strips in golden homemade breadcrumbs, baked not fried, with sweet potato wedges, peas and a lemony yoghurt dip. Like posh fish fingers.

Easy baked chicken Kievs

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
45 min
Lighter adult plate
Use half the garlic butter. Adults: a whole Kiev, lots of beans and salad, and 2–3 potatoes.
Tip
This replaces the shop-bought frozen Kievs the family uses.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives (fresh or dried)
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 slices of bread (a day or two old), whizzed into crumbs, or 100g panko
  • 750g baby new potatoes
  • 200g green beans

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Mash the butter, garlic and chives together.
  2. Cut a pocket into the thick side of each breast (not all the way through). Spoon in the butter and press closed.
  3. Dip each breast in flour, then egg, then the breadcrumbs, and put on a lined tray.
  4. Bake 25–30 min until golden and the chicken is white all the way through, with no pink.
  5. Meanwhile boil the potatoes 15 min, adding the green beans for the last 5 min.
  6. Slice the Kievs for the twins and let them cool, because the butter inside is very hot.
