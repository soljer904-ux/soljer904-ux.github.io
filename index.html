Steak (grown-ups) & breaded chicken (kids) with salad & Tenderstem

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
30 min
Type
Beef
Tip
Lighter plate: a smaller steak, extra salad and Tenderstem.

Ingredients

  • 2 Lidl steaks (sirloin or rump, about 400g total), for the grown-ups
  • 1 pack shop-bought breaded chicken mini fillets or goujons (about 400g), for the kids
  • Tenderstem broccoli (about 200g)
  • 1 head broccoli (optional extra greens)
  • Salad leaves, cherry tomatoes, cucumber
  • Butter and a garlic clove for basting (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven for the breaded chicken and cook it for the time on the pack until piping hot all the way through.
  2. Steam or boil the Tenderstem (and broccoli if using) for about 5 min until just tender.
  3. Take the steaks out of the fridge 20 min before cooking. Pat dry and season.
  4. Heat a heavy pan until very hot. Cook the steaks 2–3 min each side for medium-rare, adding butter and garlic to baste at the end.
  5. Rest 5 min while you toss the salad. Serve steak with salad and greens; kids get the breaded chicken with the same sides.

Lidl steak, pan-fried, for the grown-ups; shop-bought breaded chicken for the kids; a big crunchy salad and Tenderstem broccoli. Leaner sides than the usual wedges.

Chicken & leek pie (pastry lid only)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
50 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a big scoop of filling with a small piece of pastry, and a pile of greens. Skip the potatoes.
Tip
The filling can be made in the morning and kept in the fridge. Just add the pastry and bake later.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts (about 600g), diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 150g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 chicken stock cube in 200ml boiling water
  • 300ml semi-skimmed milk
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 1 sheet (320g) ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Broccoli or green beans to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Fry the chicken in the oil in a large pan for 5 min until white all over.
  3. Add the leeks and mushrooms and cook 5 min until soft.
  4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 min.
  5. Slowly stir in the stock, then the milk. Simmer 5 min, stirring, until thick. Add the thyme and peas.
  6. Tip into an ovenproof dish. Lay the pastry over the top, trim the edges, brush with egg and cut a small slit.
  7. Bake 25 min until golden. Serve with greens (and mash or new potatoes for the kids).

Chicken fajitas (homemade spice mix)

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
25 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a fajita bowl, with chicken and peppers on lettuce, plus yoghurt and salsa and one wrap or none.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes or hot sauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts, sliced into strips
  • 2 peppers and 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Spice mix: 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 8–10 small tortilla wraps
  • 1 little gem lettuce, 2 tomatoes, ¼ cucumber
  • Grated cheddar and Greek yoghurt
  • 1 lime

Method

  1. Mix the spices in a bowl and toss the chicken in them.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken 6–8 min until cooked through.
  3. Add the peppers and onion and cook 5 min until soft. Squeeze over half the lime.
  4. Chop the lettuce, tomatoes and cucumber.
  5. Warm the wraps in the microwave for 20 seconds.
  6. Put everything on the table and let everyone build their own.

Homemade fish fingers, wedges & peas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a double portion of fish, a few wedges, and peas plus salad.
Tip
Take the fish out of the freezer the night before and defrost it in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 4–5 frozen skinless cod or haddock fillets (about 500g), defrosted
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • 800g potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g frozen peas

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the wedges in 1 tbsp oil on a tray and bake 35 min.
  2. Pat the fish dry with kitchen roll and cut into finger strips.
  3. Put flour, egg and panko (mixed with parsley) in three bowls. Dip each strip in flour, then egg, then panko.
  4. Lay on a second lined tray and drizzle with the rest of the oil.
  5. Bake for the last 12–15 min of the wedges' time, until golden and the fish flakes.
  6. Cook the peas. Serve with lemon wedges.

Hidden-veg spaghetti bolognese

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Lighter adult plate
Adults: a big ladle of sauce over a small nest of pasta (or courgette ribbons) with a side salad. The sauce is the protein, so be generous with it.
Adult add-on
Chilli flakes stirred into adult bowls.
Tip
Make double and freeze half. It's the family's most-cooked meal.

Ingredients

  • 500g lean beef mince (5% fat)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 courgette
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 300g spaghetti
  • Grated cheddar to serve

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion, grate the carrot and courgette, crush the garlic.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pan and soften the onion for 5 min.
  3. Add the mince and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook 5–6 min until browned all over.
  4. Stir in the garlic, carrot and courgette and cook 2 min.
  5. Add the tomatoes, purée, crumbled stock cube and oregano. Simmer 20 min with the lid off, stirring now and then.
  6. Meanwhile cook the spaghetti as the packet says. Snap it shorter or cut it up for the twins.
  7. Serve topped with a little cheese.

One-tray chicken & veg traybake

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
50 min (10 min hands-on)
Lighter adult plate
Adults: 2 thighs, lots of veg and broccoli, and a few potatoes.
Tip
It's all one tray, which makes it the easiest Thursday meal.

Ingredients

  • 8 boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • Autumn/winter: 600g potatoes or sweet potatoes, 2 carrots, 1 red onion
  • Spring/summer: 600g new potatoes, 1 courgette, 1 pepper, 1 red onion
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried mixed herbs
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp paprika

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Chop the veg (not the broccoli) into chunks and spread on a large tray.
  3. Add the chicken, oil, herbs, garlic and paprika and toss everything together with your hands.
  4. Roast 25 min.
  5. Add the broccoli, stir and roast 15–20 min more, until the chicken is cooked through and the veg is golden.

Homemade flatbread pizzas

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
20 min
Type
Other
Tip
Lighter plate: a thinner wrap base loaded with veg and ham, with a big salad on the side.

Ingredients

  • 7 flatbreads or large wholemeal wraps
  • 300ml passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 3 balls mozzarella, torn (or 225g grated)
  • Toppings: 150g sliced ham, 2 peppers, a small tin of sweetcorn, 150g mushrooms, cherry tomatoes
  • 2 bags Italian style salad leaves and a cucumber for a big side salad

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and put two trays in to heat.
  2. Simmer the passata with the garlic and oregano for 5 min until a bit thicker.
  3. Spread a thin layer of sauce on each flatbread, then add mozzarella and toppings.
  4. Slide onto the hot trays and bake 8–10 min until bubbling and crisp at the edges.
  5. Cut into strips for the twins and serve with salad.

Make-your-own pizza night on flatbreads with a quick homemade passata sauce. Scaled up for 7, and everyone can add their own toppings.

Easy baked chicken Kievs

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
45 min
Lighter adult plate
Use half the garlic butter. Adults: a whole Kiev, lots of beans and salad, and 2–3 potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 40g butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives (fresh or dried)
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 slices of bread (a day or two old), whizzed into crumbs, or 100g panko
  • 750g baby new potatoes
  • 200g green beans

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Mash the butter, garlic and chives together.
  2. Cut a pocket into the thick side of each breast (not all the way through). Spoon in the butter and press closed.
  3. Dip each breast in flour, then egg, then the breadcrumbs, and put on a lined tray.
  4. Bake 25–30 min until golden and the chicken is white all the way through, with no pink.
  5. Meanwhile boil the potatoes 15 min, adding the green beans for the last 5 min.
  6. Slice the Kievs for the twins and let them cool, because the butter inside is very hot.

Roast pork loin with roast potatoes, veg & apple gravy

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
2 hr
Type
Pork
Tip
Lighter plate: trim the fat and crackling, have extra veg and fewer potatoes. Leftover pork makes good sandwiches on Monday.

Ingredients

  • About 1.6kg rolled pork loin joint, skin scored (Lidl)
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced, and a few rosemary sprigs
  • 1 onion and 1 large carrot, chopped (bed for the pork)
  • 1 Bramley apple, peeled and sliced
  • 1.4kg potatoes, peeled and halved
  • 4 carrots, cut into batons
  • 400g Tenderstem broccoli
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp plain flour and 500ml chicken stock (for the gravy)
  • Olive oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 230°C (210°C fan). Poke slivers of garlic and rosemary into small cuts in the meat, rub salt into the skin.
  2. Sit the pork on the chopped onion, carrot and apple in a roasting tin. Roast 15 min, then turn down to 180°C (160°C fan) for 1 hr 30 min.
  3. Par-boil the potatoes for 8 min, shake them up, toss in a little oil and roast for the last 50 min, turning once.
  4. Rest the pork for 15–20 min while you boil the carrots, Tenderstem and peas.
  5. Make the gravy in the tin: stir the flour into the veg and apple, add the stock, simmer 8–10 min and strain.
  6. Carve and serve with the potatoes, veg and gravy.

A proper Sunday roast for 7: rolled pork loin with crackling, roast potatoes, carrots, Tenderstem and peas, with a quick apple gravy made in the tin.

Creamy salmon, leek & potato traybake

Serves
5 (2 adults, 3 children)
Time
40 min
Type
Fish
Tip
Lighter plate: a whole fillet with plenty of peas and Tenderstem, and fewer potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 500g baby potatoes, thickly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 leeks, halved, washed and sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200ml reduced-fat crème fraîche (instead of the recipe's double cream)
  • 2 tbsp chives, chopped (fresh or dried)
  • 5 skinless salmon fillets
  • To serve: frozen peas and Tenderstem broccoli

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Boil the potatoes for 8 min, drain and leave to steam-dry for a few minutes.
  2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and some seasoning in a large roasting tin and roast for 20 min, tossing halfway.
  3. Meanwhile, fry the leeks in the rest of the oil over a medium heat for 5 min until soft, then stir in the garlic for 1 min.
  4. Turn the heat down low and stir in the crème fraîche with about 150ml hot water until smooth and just bubbling (keep it gentle so it doesn't split). Stir through the chives.
  5. Heat the grill to high. Pour the creamy leeks over the potatoes, sit the salmon on top and grill for 7–8 min until the salmon flakes easily.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the peas and Tenderstem, and serve on the side.

Golden roast baby potatoes in a mild, creamy leek sauce with salmon on top, served with peas and Tenderstem. Made with reduced-fat crème fraîche instead of double cream, and scaled up for 5.
