Daily flexibility + partner

Daily: 5–8 min easy mobility. Optional partner with Isla (7): supervise. Isla uses no kettlebell — bodyweight or 1–2 kg bottle; knee/step push-ups.

  1. 3-min warm-up — 10 hinges · 10 bridges · 10 air squats · 10 cat-cows
  2. Isla swing tip — one slow hinge with bottle at arm's length before round 1
  3. Swings — alternate 5 each per minute × 10 (12 kg · Isla bottle/none)
  4. Squats — 3 × 12 each · one works while the other rests
  5. Push-up / row relay — one each, swap · 3 rounds

Use the Partner timer preset.
