    UK Firework Code · safety first

    Strobe Surge: CAUTION — Strobe Effect. Warn anyone sensitive to flashing lights; omit it if needed. Wheel B is optional: skip it if you only have one wheel.

    • Spectators at least 8 m AND every greater labelled distance (possibly 15 m or 25 m). If your garden cannot meet it, omit that item. Never assume 8 m is enough.
    • Never return to a lit firework. Treat a misfire as live. Wait at least 30 minutes, then soak only when manufacturer/supplier guidance says it is safe; never approach, relight or troubleshoot it during the show.
    • Only connect igniters to the DB04R remote if positively identified as suitable and fitted to both manufacturers' instructions. Orange wire colour is not identification. Otherwise hand-light everything and use remote cues only as timing prompts.
    • Four independent starts, one item per output. No fuse modification, chaining, multiple items per output or in-show rewiring. Buttons 5–12 add no outputs. Test mapping/range only with approved inert testers; continuity is not a safety certificate.
    • Sober adult firer; children remain supervised behind the barrier. Secure candles (never handheld), use specified stable rocket tubes (never bottles), mount wheel as labelled. Pets indoors, water available, stock closed away from ignition.
    • Never rush lighting to the music. Unlimited holds. No one approaches a failed item to reach another. Ordinary 23:00 GMT cutoff applies tonight, subject to local rules.
